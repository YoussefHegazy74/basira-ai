بصيرة · brand preview

Logo and color directions

Three logo concepts and three palettes for «بصيرة». The website and every mock UI stay Arabic; the notes are in English. Nothing in the app has been changed yet.

Arabic wordmark onlyNo taglineNo goldNo religious symbols

What I took from the references

  • Yaqeen Institute: clear research hierarchy and visible citations. Avoid its saturated blue and orange.
  • Tarteel: monochrome confidence and one simple geometric mark. Avoid relying on a large lifestyle photo as the brand.
  • Qalam Institute: warmth and community. Avoid its generic bright-blue call to action.
  • Linear: layered dark surfaces, quiet borders, one restrained accent. Avoid neon or AI-style gradients.
  • Notion: approachable warmth and a single soft highlight. Avoid playful illustrations here; this brand needs more gravity.
  • IxDF logo principles: simple, memorable, scalable, versatile, and tested in one color.
  • WCAG: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and UI edges.
  • Microsoft Arabic typography + IBM Plex Sans Arabic: respect contextual forms, joins, dots, and diacritics; never force Latin metrics onto Arabic.

Clichés avoided: eye, crescent, mosque, prayer beads, gold or metal, sparkles, robots, brains, circuits, generic chat bubbles, default “tech blue”, pure black, and pure white.

Color directions

All three use a warm sand and ink base, with a different accent family. Every sample below uses Arabic UI text. The ratios are measured, not estimated.
